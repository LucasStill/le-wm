## Headline findings

### 1. **In-distribution and OOD trade off across (H, S, W)**
- **E2 (H=32) is best in-distribution**: 0.597 ± 0.025
- **E6probe (S=10) is best OOD**: 0.345 ± 0.044
- The E2-vs-E6 difference is robust across multi-seed; this is the cleanest
  in-dist↔OOD tradeoff in the campaign.

### 2. **Larger S consistently helps OOD generalization (JEPA)**
S=1 (E1, OOD=0.23) → S=5 (E3, OOD=0.28) → S=10 (E6, OOD=0.35). The trend
holds even though E6 is only a 1-epoch probe. Trajectory of full-training
E6 is open question (E7probe at S=20 will tell us).

### 3. **Larger H helps in-distribution but not OOD (JEPA)**
H=8 (E5, in=0.40) → H=16 (E1, in=0.45) → H=32 (E2, in=0.60). Mostly
monotonic in-dist. No corresponding monotone trend in OOD.

### 4. **Predictor architecture has a small in-dist effect at H=16, big effect at H=32 OOD**
- H=16: E1 (JEPA, 0.45 in-dist / 0.23 OOD) ≈ L1 (LSTM, 0.56 / 0.33).
- H=32: E2 (JEPA, 0.60 / 0.33) ≈ L_big (LSTM+W=4, 0.56 / 0.33);
        L2 (LSTM no W) collapses OOD to -0.014.
- **Interpretation**: H=32 alone is a dangerous regime for AR-LSTM
  encoders without a temporal aggregator (W>1); JEPA tolerates it fine.

### 5. **W>1 (TemporalAggregator) protects OOD without lifting in-dist**
L_big (W=4) has same in-dist as L1 but recovers L2's OOD collapse.
W>1 acts as a regularizer against H=32-induced OOD specialization.

### 6. ⚠ **Aggregate OOD numbers hide a held-out-archetype split**
*(OT's discovery, 2026-04-27)* Per-archetype breakdown of L1 × test_hard:
- B_fan_booster (n=41,787): Pearson = -0.117 ← arch held out from train
- C_turbine, D_balanced: ≈ 0
- A_compressor: ZERO windows in test_hard

The "L1 OOD 0.358" headline number is dominated by B_fan_booster signal.
**Methodology consequence**: per-archetype OOD must be reported. Aggregate
OOD numbers conflate distribution shift with archetype mix.

### 7. **Probe pipeline matters: in-training vs eval_sweep gap is 2-3×**
Default `n_subsample=30000` understated encoder Pearson by 2-3×
(E1 in-training 0.25 vs eval_sweep at 770K windows 0.45). Bumped to
200K in `lewm.yaml`. **All future runs should use the calibrated
pipeline for headline numbers**, in-training as a coarse training-time
monitor only.

### 8. **Single-seed Pearson is unreliable; ±0.2 swings are common**
- E5 in-dist std = 0.218 (massive!)
- E1 test_hard std = 0.180
- ALL numbers in pre-2026-04-27 single-seed reports must be re-quoted
  with multi-seed mean ± std before publication.
- Probe-init seed bug: seed=1 produces NaN for most configs (degenerate
  init); aggregator filters. Worth a fix in `eval_sweep.train_probe`.

### 9. **Action-RUL probe is consistently noise-floor across all configs**
Pearson ≈ 0, R² ≤ 0 universally. Class-imbalanced (rare maintenance
events). **Drop from headline tables** unless we get one publishable
result via different probe head or longer training. Currently
deprioritized.

### 10. **Task 2 (delta-HI velocity) similarly returns NaN on AR-LSTM**
OT noted: ΔHI target too small for the probe size (test predictions
are constant → pearsonr undefined). Either drop it from the benchmark
or rescale ΔHI before probing.
